FleetFinch

A software suite designed to bring modern functionality to iOS 9.3.5 and iOS 9.3.6 devices

Supported Devices
  • iPhone 4s Apple A5, 512 MB RAM, 960×640
  • iPad 2 Apple A5, 512 MB RAM, 1024×768
  • iPad (3rd generation) Apple A5X, 1 GB RAM, 2048×1536
  • iPad mini (1st generation) Apple A5, 512 MB RAM, 1024×768
  • iPod touch (5th generation) Apple A5, 512 MB RAM, 1136×640
